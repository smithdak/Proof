Surface: Proof Console (web), the human operating surface over the Proof
collaboration server. Mode: Operate - the visitor completes governed work
(review, validate, approve, commit, release, verify).

Audience: platform/content-engineering teams and content operators
supervising agent-proposed changes; agent builders auditing what ran under
which authority. Job: move a ChangeSet through its lifecycle with evidence
beside every step, then verify the resulting proof. Action: approve and
commit are the consequential moments; everything else is reading and
checking. Proof/content: the register itself - diffs, validation findings,
verification root checklists, signed envelopes. Constraints: nine-route
HTTP surface, human-only approvals, immutable published state, WCAG 2.2 AA,
keyboard-first.

Direction: notarial ledger / audit register (assigned candidate 4, seed
8fe727c9, code-led build). Laid-paper ground, iron-gall ink, prussian
ruling hairlines as the only chrome, rubber-stamp status marks, one
reserved consequential ink for irreversible actions, mono identifiers,
small-caps labels. Memorable moment: the release verification report
reading as a stamped, six-root receipt.
