# Produce the evidence-backed Sitecore pain-point register

[Back to the work map](../map.md)

## Outcome

A durable, evidence-cited register of Sitecore-class operational pain points
that Proof is designed to eliminate. Each pain point names the observed
Sitecore behavior, its enterprise cost, Proof's redesign intent, and the
measurable outcome that proves elimination.

## Why now

The capability canon (P-0026) names what Proof must do. This register names why
Proof's approach wins: it converts competitive evidence into design pressure so
implementation waves are driven by measurable elimination of real pain, not
feature parity.

## Scope

- Research and catalog Sitecore pain points across: authoring friction, content
  operations latency, workflow opacity, publishing complexity, upgrade fragility,
  licensing/operational cost, developer experience, and AI integration gaps.
- Cite public evidence for each entry (documentation, changelogs, community
  reports, pricing pages) — no uncited claims.
- For each pain point, state Proof's redesign commitment and the measurable
  acceptance condition that proves elimination.
- This register is a living document; it is updated as evidence changes, not
  rewritten wholesale.

## Non-goals

- No implementation work.
- No architecture change.
- No performance benchmark specification (P-0029).
- No UI or console work.

## Acceptance evidence

- A committed pain-point register in `docs/product/` with citation-backed
  entries and per-entry Proof redesign commitment.
- Each entry names a measurable outcome Proof commits to.
- Project-owner acceptance recorded per work-item protocol.
